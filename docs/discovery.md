# Client discovery

_Interview with the owners, Sep 29, 2026. Terms follow `CONTEXT.md`._

> **Public repo rule:** no business, customer, student, school or supplier names, and no prices here. Write "School A", "Supplier 1", and ranges, not figures.

## Interview questions (~45 min, with the notebook open)

### Walk me through one real order (start here, pick a recent one)

1. How did it arrive: Messenger, a Rep, walk-in, the school itself?
2. What did you write in the notebook, and in what order? What happened next, step by step, until it was released?
3. Who touched it along the way (you, staff, supplier, courier)?

### Who orders (checks Customer / Rep / School)

4. Do students ever contact you directly, or does a Rep handle everything?
5. Who pays, and when (deposit, full, per student, per school)?
6. After ordering, who asks "is it ready yet?", and how often?

### Batches and dates (checks Batch / Due date / Overdue)

7. Do you think in schools/batches or in individual orders? Can one student's order be in a different batch from their classmates'?
8. Who sets the date you promise, and what is it tied to (graduation, a school event)?
9. Does the supplier give you their own date? Has a supplier being late ever made you late?
10. What's the worst thing that went wrong in the last year? (Late batch, lost order, wrong size, wrong engraving.)

### Production (checks Stage / Status)

11. What are the steps between "order received" and "ready"? Name them in your words.
12. Which steps are done by a supplier, and which in-house?
13. What would you _not_ want a student to see about an order's progress?

### Products and options

14. Rings, pins, dog tags: what can a customer choose on each (size, stone, metal, engraving, year)?
15. Does each school have its own design? Does it change each year?
16. Where do sizes and engravings go wrong most often?

### People and devices

17. Who will use the system: both of you, staff? Should anyone see less than others (prices, suppliers)?
18. Phone or laptop? English, Filipino, or mixed on the screens?
19. How many orders in a busy month? How many in the notebook right now that are still open?

### Close

20. If this only did one thing well by Oct 18, what should it be?
21. What would make you stop using it and go back to the notebook?
